Domain 1. Randomization process

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
1.1 Was a random component used in the sequence generation process?PYThe article states patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio but does not describe the specific method. As a large phase 3 trial published in a major journal, it is probably adequate."patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio" (Methods)文章提到1:1随机分配，但未描述具体方法。作为大型3期试验，可能使用了适当的随机序列生成方法。
1.2 Was the allocation sequence appropriately concealed?PNNo description of allocation concealment (e.g., central randomization, opaque envelopes). Given the open-label design and lack of detail, it is probably not concealed.No explicit mention of allocation concealment in the text.未描述分配隐藏方法（如中心随机、密封信封）。由于开放标签设计且无细节，可能未实施分配隐藏。
1.3 Were there baseline imbalances that suggest a problem with the randomization process?NTable 1 shows baseline characteristics are well balanced between groups, and the authors explicitly state this."Baseline characteristics were well balanced between the two treatment groups (Table 1)." (Results)表1显示两组基线特征平衡，作者明确说明平衡良好。无迹象表明随机化过程有问题。
Note for 1.1&1.2: "patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio" (Methods). The specific method of sequence generation is not described, but as a large phase 3 trial published in a major journal, it is probably adequate (PY). No description of allocation concealment (e.g., central randomization, opaque envelopes). Given the open-label design, it is probably not concealed (PN).
Note for 1.3: "Baseline characteristics were well balanced between the two treatment groups (Table 1)." (Results). No substantial imbalances that would indicate problems with randomization.

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
