Overall Risk of Bias Judgement: Low risk of bias

This is a well-conducted, albeit open-label, randomized trial. The primary outcome (pCR) is objective and was assessed by blinded, independent central review, protecting it from the introduction of bias. The analysis was a robust, pre-specified intention-to-treat approach, which mitigates concerns about missing outcome data and deviations from the protocol. The overall risk of bias is low.
