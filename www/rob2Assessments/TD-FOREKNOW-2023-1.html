2. Domain 1. Randomization process

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
1.1 Was the allocation sequence random?PYPermuted block randomization (block size 4) is a valid random component."Eligible patients were randomly assigned ... at a ratio of 1:1 by permuted block randomization with block size of 4."使用排列区组随机化，序列生成合理。
1.2 Was the allocation sequence concealed until participants were enrolled and assigned to interventions?PNOpaque sealed envelopes were used, but this method is not considered as robust as central randomization; some risk of subversion exists."The treatment allocation was implemented via opaque, sealed envelopes."使用不透明密封信封进行分配隐藏，但非中心随机化，可能存在一定风险。
Note for 1.1 & 1.2"Eligible patients were randomly assigned to receive neoadjuvant camrelizumab plus chemotherapy or chemotherapy alone at a ratio of 1:1 by permuted block randomization with block size of 4. The treatment allocation was implemented via opaque, sealed envelopes." Block randomization is adequate (PY). Opaque envelopes are a less secure method than central allocation, thus PN for concealment.
1.3 Were there baseline imbalances that suggest a problem with the randomization process?NBaseline characteristics were well balanced (Table 1)."Demographic and disease characteristics at baseline were generally well balanced between treatment groups (Table 1)."基线特征均衡，无问题。
Note for 1.3"Demographic and disease characteristics at baseline were generally well balanced between treatment groups (Table 1)." No substantial prognostic imbalances noted.
Domain 1 JudgementLow riskAdequate randomization with minor concerns about allocation concealment, but baseline balance supports low risk.

5. Domain 4. Measurement of the outcome

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
4.1 Was the method of measuring the outcome inappropriate?NpCR defined by absence of viable tumor in primary tumor and lymph nodes, a standard, appropriate measure."Pathologic complete response was defined as an absence of viable tumor cells in the surgical specimens from the primary tumor and all sampled regional lymph nodes."pCR定义标准、合适。
Note for 4.1"Pathologic complete response was defined as an absence of viable tumor cells in the surgical specimens from the primary tumor and all sampled regional lymph nodes." This is the accepted standard in neoadjuvant NSCLC trials.
4.2 Could measurement or ascertainment of the outcome have differed between intervention groups?NSame pathological assessment methods for both groups.(Implied by consistent definition and central review.)两组测量方法相同。
Note for 4.2No indication of differential ascertainment; surgical specimens were evaluated using same criteria.
4.3 Were outcome assessors aware of the intervention received?NAlthough trial was open-label, pathological assessment is typically performed by pathologists blinded to treatment. The article does not specify blinding of pathologists, but given the objective nature of pCR and standard practice, we assume assessors were unaware. (If no information, could answer NI, but in this context we infer low risk.)(Not explicitly stated; inference.)病理评估者可能对分组不知情（客观指标）。
Note for 4.3The manuscript does not explicitly state whether pathologists were blinded. Given pCR is an objective histologic assessment, risk of bias from knowledge is low.
4.4 (conditional) Could assessment be influenced by knowledge of intervention?NANot applicable because 4.3 = N.—不适用。
4.5 (conditional) Is it likely that assessment was influenced?NANot applicable.—不适用。
