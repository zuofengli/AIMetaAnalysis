2. Domain 1 – Randomisation process

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
1.1 Was the allocation sequence random?YCentral randomisation with interactive response technology and block randomisation.“Randomisation was done centrally using an interactive response technology system and was stratified by disease stage, PD-L1 expression, histology, and geographical region in blocks of four.”分配序列由中心交互式响应系统生成，包含随机成分，充分随机。
1.2 Was the allocation concealed until participants were enrolled?YCentral randomisation ensured allocation concealment.“Randomisation was done centrally using an interactive response technology system”中心随机化保证了分配隐藏，研究者无法预知分组。
1.3 Did baseline differences suggest a problem with the randomisation process?NBaseline table shows balanced characteristics; authors state groups were balanced.“baseline demographic and disease characteristics were balanced between the two groups (table 1).”基线特征均衡，未提示随机化存在问题。
Domain 1 judgementLow riskRandomisation adequate, concealment ensured, baseline balanced.—随机化过程低风险
