2. Domain 1 – Randomization Process

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
1.1 Was the allocation sequence random? Y Minimisation with a 15% random component; central IVRS used for randomisation. The randomisation list was generated by an independent vendor (Almac).
"Based on a minimisation technique with a random allocation component to ensure 15% of completely random assignments, eligible participants were randomly assigned (1:1) using a central interactive voice-response system (Almac Clinical Technologies, Souderton, PA, USA)..."
 分配序列采用含随机元素的极小化方法，由独立供应商通过中央IVRS执行，充分随机。
1.2 Was the allocation concealed until participants were enrolled and assigned? Y Central IVRS ensures allocation concealment. Randomisation numbers were scrambled.
"...randomisation numbers were scrambled to ensure concealment of treatment assignment."
 使用中央交互式语音应答系统并打乱随机编号，分配隐藏充分。
1.3 Did baseline differences suggest a problem? N Table 1 demonstrates balanced baseline demographics and disease characteristics. Authors explicitly state baseline balance.
"Baseline characteristics were generally balanced between treatment groups in the overall and PD-L1 TPS of 50% or greater populations (table 1)."
 基线特征在两组间均衡（见表1），无问题。
Domain 1 Judgement Low risk of bias — Central randomisation with IVRS, adequate concealment, balanced baseline characteristics.

3. Domain 2 – Deviations from Intended Interventions (ITT effect)

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
2.1 Were participants aware of their assigned intervention? PN Triple-blind, placebo-controlled design. Participants were formally masked. Immune-related AEs (hypothyroidism 21% vs 5%) could cause some functional unblinding, but formal blinding was maintained.
"Participants, investigators, and analysts were masked to treatment assignment..."
 三盲、安慰剂对照试验。受试者不知分组；免疫相关AE可能导致部分功能性破盲，但正式盲态维持。
2.2 Were carers/people delivering interventions aware? PN Investigators and those delivering interventions were masked. Local pharmacists knew assignments but did not deliver interventions.
"Local pharmacists were aware of assignments."
 研究者及给药人员设盲；仅当地药师知晓分组但不参与给药。
2.3 Were important non-protocol interventions balanced? (ITT context: deviations due to trial context?) NA Not applicable — both 2.1 and 2.2 are PN, so no concern about trial-context deviations.
2.4 Were deviations likely to affect the outcome? NA Not applicable — 2.3 is NA.
2.5 Were deviations balanced between groups? NA Not applicable — 2.4 is NA.
2.6 Was an appropriate ITT analysis used? Y All randomised participants (n=1177) included in the ITT analysis. Only 1 participant excluded (no consent). Crossover not permitted.
"Efficacy was assessed in the intention-to-treat (ITT) population, defined as all participants randomly assigned to a treatment group."
 分析使用全部随机化的ITT人群（1177例），仅1例因无知情同意排除。不允许交叉。
2.7 Potential for substantial impact of failure to analyse as randomised? NA Not applicable — 2.6 is Y (appropriate analysis used).
Domain 2 Judgement Low risk of bias — Triple-blind, ITT analysis including all randomised participants, no crossovers permitted.

5. Domain 4 – Measurement of the Outcome

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
4.1 Was the measurement method inappropriate? N DFS assessed by RECIST v1.1 — standard, validated method for oncology trials. Appropriate for this context.
"Disease-free survival was defined as time from randomisation to locoregional or metastatic recurrence assessed per RECIST version 1.1 by investigator review..."
 RECIST v1.1是评估NSCLC复发的标准方法，适用于该结局。
4.2 Could measurement differ between groups? PN Identical CT/MRI imaging schedule for both groups throughout follow-up. Same assessment protocol.
"Contrast-enhanced chest and upper abdomen CT scans were done... every 12 weeks thereafter for year 1, every 6 months during years 2 and 3, annually in years 4 and 5..."
 两组采用完全相同的影像学随访计划，测量方法无差异。
4.3 Were outcome assessors aware of intervention? N Investigators assessing DFS were masked. Triple-blind design ensured outcome assessors did not know group assignment.
"Participants, investigators, and analysts were masked to treatment assignment..."
 三盲设计，评估DFS的研究者不知分组。
4.4 Could assessment be influenced by knowledge of intervention? NA Not applicable — 4.3 is N (assessors were masked).
4.5 Is it likely assessment was influenced? NA Not applicable — 4.4 is NA.
Domain 4 Judgement Low risk of bias — Standard outcome measurement (RECIST v1.1), identical assessment schedule, triple-blind masking of assessors.

6. Domain 5 – Selection of the Reported Result

QuestionAnswerExplanation & EvidenceOriginal QuoteChinese Explanation
5.1 Were data analysed per pre-specified plan? Y Protocol with SAP available in appendix. Second interim analysis was pre-specified. Trial registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT02504372).
"The statistical analysis plan is in the protocol (appendix)." "Here we report results of the second interim analysis, prespecified to occur when approximately 118 disease-free survival events had occurred in the PD-L1 TPS of 50% or greater population."
 统计分析计划在方案附录中预先规定；第二次期中分析为预先设定。试验在ClinicalTrials.gov注册。
5.2 Selected from multiple eligible outcome measurements? PN DFS is a single, well-defined primary endpoint. The pre-specified dual primary endpoints (overall and PD-L1≥50% populations) are both reported.
"The dual primary endpoints were disease-free survival in the overall population and in the PD-L1 TPS of 50% or greater population..."
 DFS定义明确、单一，预先设定的双主要终点均有报告，未选择性报告测量方式。
5.3 Selected from multiple eligible analyses? PN Primary analysis used the pre-specified multivariate Cox model. Pre-specified sensitivity analysis (censoring pre-randomisation recurrences) also reported. No evidence of selection.
"We assessed treatment comparisons using... a multivariate Cox proportional hazards model adjusted for the stratification factors at randomisation and the additional factors of histology... and smoking status..."
 主要分析使用预先规定的多变量Cox模型。敏感性分析亦按方案预先规定。无选择性报告分析方法的迹象。
Domain 5 Judgement Low risk of bias — Pre-registered, pre-specified analysis plan followed; all primary endpoints reported as planned.
